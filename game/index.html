Dusk District · Sector 9

HARBOUR CITY

The sun has gone down behind the towers and the avenues are full of traffic. Take a car off the kerb, run four jobs for people who pay cash and ask nothing, and keep one eye on the mirror: this city counts your stars.

WASD move Shift sprint Space jump / brake F in / out of a car Click fire Drag orbit
Missions Four jobs on the amber beacon: Hot Wire, Docks Run, Packages, The Vault. Hot Wire pays only when the lifted car is standing still inside the drop ring.
Wanted system Shots and shunts add heat. At two stars the radio sends two to four cars that ram you; stay out of sight for twenty seconds and a star comes off, and at zero the street is empty again. Red dots on the minimap are units.
